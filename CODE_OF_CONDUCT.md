# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Discuss code, not the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith and is short on time.
- Give feedback about the work: what is wrong, why it matters, what would be better.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change without that being a judgement of you.
- Remember that most people here are not writing in their first language. Read for meaning, not for
  grammar.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer at
[github.com/phuthuycoding](https://github.com/phuthuycoding). Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments and code that breach this document, and may block
accounts that keep doing so. Enforcement is proportionate: a first lapse usually gets a word, not a
ban.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits — and anywhere someone
is representing the project.
